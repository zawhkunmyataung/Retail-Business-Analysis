## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Executive Summary

![Executive Summary](Executive%20Summary.png)

#### What this page shows

The Executive Summary provides an overview of the company's overall sales and profitability performance, with a focus on revenue, profit, returns, regional performance, brands, and changes over time.

#### Key Findings

- Total revenue reached **$1.76M**, while total profit was **$1.05M**.
- Profit margin was **59.67%**.
- **North West** generated approximately **$848K**, making it the highest-revenue sales region.
- The **USA** generated approximately **$1.18M in revenue** and **$703K in profit**.
- **Hermanos** was the highest-revenue brand shown on the page.
- **Tell Tale Fresh Lima Beans** had the highest sales volume among the products analysed.
- The monthly return rate remained relatively stable, ranging from approximately **0.78% to 0.95%**.

#### Business Interpretation

Revenue and profit are concentrated in specific geographic markets, particularly the USA and North West region. This suggests that regional performance should be examined further to understand the customer, product, and store characteristics associated with stronger results.

The relatively low and stable return rate suggests that returns were not a major issue during the period analysed, although continued monitoring can help identify unusual changes.
