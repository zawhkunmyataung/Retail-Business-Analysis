# Retail Business Analysis

## 📊 Business Overview

This project analyses retail business performance across sales, profitability, products, customers, stores, and geographic markets.

The analysis focuses on identifying the key contributors to revenue and profit, understanding customer purchasing patterns, comparing regional and store performance, evaluating product performance, and monitoring return trends.

The analysis addresses key business questions such as:

- Which regions and countries contribute most to revenue and profit?
- Which products and brands contribute most to overall performance?
- Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
- How does performance vary across store types and individual stores?
- How do product characteristics relate to sales performance?
- How does the return rate change over time?
- Which areas require further investigation to understand performance differences?

---

## 🎯 Key Business Findings

### Overall Performance

The business generated approximately **$1.76M in revenue** and **$1.05M in profit**, resulting in a **59.67% profit margin**.

The return rate remained relatively low and stable throughout the period analysed, ranging from approximately **0.78% to 0.95%**.

Overall performance indicates strong profitability, while the relatively low return rate suggests that returns represented a small proportion of overall sales activity.

---

### 🌎 Regional & Geographic Performance

**North West** was the highest-revenue sales region, generating approximately **$848K**, which represents almost half of total revenue.

The **USA** generated approximately **$1.18M in revenue** and **$703K in profit**, making it the largest contributor among the countries analysed.

The concentration of revenue in specific geographic markets indicates that business performance is not evenly distributed across regions.

Further analysis could examine the customer, product, and store characteristics associated with stronger regional performance.

---

### 🏷️ Product & Brand Performance

**Hermanos** was the highest-revenue brand shown in the analysis.

**Tell Tale Fresh Lima Beans** was identified as the leading product based on the dashboard's best-selling metric.

The analysis also identifies the **bottom-performing products by sales volume**, providing an opportunity to investigate differences in product demand, profitability, pricing, and product mix.

Product characteristics showed that:

- **Regular products:** 64.64% of sales
- **Low-Fat products:** 35.36% of sales
- **Recyclable products:** 56.08% of sales
- **Non-Recyclable products:** 43.92% of sales

Regular products accounted for a larger share of sales than Low-Fat products, indicating differences in purchasing patterns across product categories.

---

### 👥 Customer Performance

Customer revenue varied across demographic and membership segments.

**Professional customers** generated the highest revenue among occupation groups, at approximately **$82K**.

Customers aged **55+** generated the highest revenue among the analysed age groups, at approximately **$123K**.

The analysis compares customer revenue across:

- Occupation
- Age group
- Education group
- Member card type
- Customer priority

These differences provide a basis for further customer segmentation analysis, including purchase frequency, average transaction value, and product preferences.

---

### 🏪 Store Performance

**Supermarkets** generated the highest revenue among store types, at approximately **$789.60K**.

**Store 13** was the highest-performing individual store in the Regional Performance analysis.

Performance differences across stores provide opportunities to investigate the relationship between:

- Store type
- Geographic location
- Product mix
- Customer composition
- Sales volume

Further analysis could help identify the characteristics associated with stronger store performance.

## 💡 Business Implications

The findings provide several areas for further business investigation:

**1. Geographic concentration**

A significant share of revenue is concentrated in the **USA and North West region**. Further analysis could investigate the customer, product, and store characteristics contributing to stronger performance in these markets.

**2. Customer segmentation**

The higher revenue generated by **Professional** customers and customers aged **55+** suggests differences in customer value across segments. Further analysis could examine purchase frequency, average transaction value, and product preferences.

**3. Product portfolio**

Differences between high- and low-performing products provide an opportunity to investigate sales volume, profitability, pricing, and product characteristics to better understand product performance.

**4. Store performance**

Variation across stores and store types suggests that location, customer composition, product mix, and sales volume may be associated with differences in store performance. Further investigation could identify patterns among stronger- and weaker-performing stores.

**5. Returns**

Although the return rate remained relatively low, monitoring monthly changes can help identify unusual increases and investigate whether specific products, stores, or regions contribute disproportionately to returns.
